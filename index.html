The new 7 Wonder:

Great Wall of China

The Great Wall stretches for thousands of kilometers across China.

Petra

Petra is an ancient city carved into rose-colored stone.

Machu Picchu

Machu Picchu sits high in the Andes Mountains of Peru.

Chichen Itza

Its famous pyramid has 365 steps, one for each day of the year.

Colosseum

The Colosseum once held tens of thousands of spectators.

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal's white marble changes appearance in different light.

Christ the Redeemer

The statue is about 38 meters tall, including its pedestal.
